# Brand and financial model basis

## Source

The supplied `Plaisir_Corrected_Business_Deck.pptx`, 35 slides, is the authoritative source for this version. The original deck remains unchanged. This page presents a leadership draft, not a verified operating business or approved forecast.

## Brand translation

- Coffee and wine, rather than a wine-only concept (slides 1–5).
- Forest green backgrounds and warm amber typography throughout. Source colours: #41654C and #F0AF49. Body text uses a slightly lighter amber for readability.
- Directly reused embedded wordmark, bottle artwork, green textures and concept renderings. Their proportions are preserved. Imagery is identified as concept material.
- Restrained sans-serif text with italic serif section accents, matching the deck's Nimbus Sans and P052 hierarchy through browser-safe font families.
- Original pillars retained: Not intimidating; Not boring; Atmosphere over wine (slide 3).
- Wine compass removed. Independent prompts for body, sweetness and occasion follow slide 10.
- No diagonal-arrow icons.

## Funding and cash proxy

The source proposes $500k–$1m with 55% fit-out, 22% equipment/technology, 13% licensing/legal and 10% working capital (slide 26). The $750k explorer default is the midpoint, not an approved raise.

Only the 10% working-capital allocation starts the operating reserve. The remaining 90% is assumed committed to the other allocations. The cash explorer adds adjustable assumptions: zero to six pre-trading months, initial sales at 60% of steady monthly sales by default, and six trading months to steady sales by default. A one-month ramp means steady sales immediately. During delay months, full fixed costs are paid and sales are zero.

Monthly change = sales × gross margin − annual fixed costs / 12. Closing reserve = starting reserve plus accumulated monthly changes. The chart includes the initial reserve and all 12 modelled months. A shortfall means the first month-end reserve below zero. It does not measure intra-month liquidity.

This is an operating cash proxy, not a complete cash forecast or a verified runway. It excludes deposits, inventory/supplier timing, tax, debt service, further capital spending, seasonal variations and contingency costs. These limits appear beside the model.

## Source inconsistencies kept visible

- Slide 22: proposed rent of $6k/month annualises to $72k, while model occupancy is $216k. The $144k difference remains unexplained. The dashboard does not silently substitute the lower rent.
- Slide 33: 220 transactions/day × $25 × 351 days = $1,930,500, rather than $833k. The demand view exposes both calculations. The implied ticket at $833k is approximately $10.79. Applying the demand result changes revenue only.
- Slide 30: three locations at $1.2–1.5m per site imply $3.6–4.5m combined sales. This is labelled arithmetic, not a validated expansion forecast.
